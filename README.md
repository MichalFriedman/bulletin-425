# Bulletin 425 — mobile edition

Open `bulletin-425.html` in a browser. The page works without a build step.
Keep `bulletin-mobile.css`, `bulletin-mobile.js`, `assets/`, and `Bulletin425.pdf` beside it when copying or hosting the edition.

`bulletin-425.original.html` is the unchanged HTML supplied at the start. The source PDF is unchanged.

The mobile edition retains Hebrew and English reading sections, uses the printed blue/white/red palette, restores the poem and English memorial text, replaces blank and misidentified photographs, and includes a reader for all 48 individual pages. The complete scholarship lists have 65 recipients per language. Wording differences between the PDF’s language editions are retained.

Verified in Chromium at widths 320, 390, 430, 768, and 1440 pixels: no horizontal page overflow in either language; all referenced images load; contents menu, language selection, saved text size, deep links, original-page navigation, and zoom work.

The source PDF has 25 sheets, mostly two-page spreads. The original-page reader splits these into 48 pages. The Hebrew 6–7 spread was reversed in the supplied layout; the shared central event notice is reached from Hebrew page 28 and English page 20.

Typography refinement: all paragraph text shares a 16px default and scales together with the reader controls. The New Year greeting uses the original composed artwork, including its lettering and seal. Double blue section rules, rectangular portraits beside the text, and the red-and-white Chinese course notice follow the supplied screenshots. Verified at 320, 390, 430, and 1024px in Hebrew and English.
